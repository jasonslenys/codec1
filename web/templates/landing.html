FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats can I convert?
We support all major video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, WebM, and more. If your video file plays on your computer, chances are we can convert it.
Is there a file size limit?
Yes, the maximum file size is 10GB. This accommodates even lengthy 4K videos. Our streaming upload and conversion process handles large files efficiently.
What is H.264 and why should I use it?
H.264 (also known as AVC) is the most widely supported video codec in the world. It offers excellent quality at reasonable file sizes and plays on virtually every device, browser, and platform. It's the standard format for web video, streaming services, and most consumer devices.
What do the quality settings mean?
The CRF (Constant Rate Factor) setting controls quality vs file size. Lower values (18-22) give higher quality but larger files. Higher values (28-35) produce smaller files with reduced quality. The default of 23 provides a good balance for most uses.
How long does conversion take?
Conversion time depends on file size, duration, and your chosen speed preset. "Ultrafast" produces results quickly but with larger file sizes. "Slow" takes longer but achieves better compression. A typical 10-minute video converts in 1-5 minutes.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. Your files are processed securely and automatically deleted from our servers after 1 hour. We never store, analyze, or share your video content. Your privacy is our priority.
